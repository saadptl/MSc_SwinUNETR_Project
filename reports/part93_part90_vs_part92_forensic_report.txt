INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The RSNA classification targets are strongly class-imbalanced, therefore raw accuracy alone is insufficient.
Macro F1, balanced accuracy, and minority-class recall are emphasized.
A reduction in raw accuracy can be acceptable if it corresponds to meaningful gains in Moderate and Severe detection.
Because Part90 and Part92 use different model architectures, their difference cannot be attributed exclusively to class weighting.
